Tally: dates log

Your dates.
Logged.

Tally is a private log for gay men to keep track of dates and partners: who, when, where and how it went. Clean stats instead of messy notes and spreadsheets.

Log

Add a date in seconds.

Start typing a name. Tally recognises partners you've met before and fills in the rest: location, roles, connection type and contact. Add protection, your rating and a note. Done.

Stats

See the numbers.

How many people this month, this year or in total. Your role split. Days since your last date. Every partner with the full timeline of your meetings.

Privacy

Stays on your phone.

No account. No servers. Everything is stored on your device and can be locked with Face ID or fingerprint. Optional backups stay in your own storage, never with us.
