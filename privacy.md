# Privacy Policy for Tally

**Last Updated: October 16, 2026**

## 1. Zero Data Collection

Tally does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal information, logs, notes, or media to our servers.

- We do not have user accounts.
- We do not have access to your database.
- We do not operate servers, so your data is never sent to us.

## 2. Local Storage

All information you enter into the App is stored locally on your mobile device using encrypted storage. You are the sole owner and custodian of your data. If you delete the App, all your data will be permanently removed from your device.

## 3. Google Sign-In and Drive Backup (Optional)

If you choose to enable backup, Tally asks you to sign in with Google and requests access only to a private app folder in your Google Drive. Tally uses this access solely to save and restore your own backup and cannot see or modify any other files in your Drive. Your backup goes directly from your device to your Google account; we never receive it.

You can delete your backup at any time from within the App. Deleting the App itself does not remove the backup from your Google Drive.

Tally's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 4. Biometric Authentication (FaceID / TouchID)

Tally offers an optional layer of security via biometric authentication.

- The authentication process is handled entirely by the operating system (iOS/Android).
- The App never accesses or stores your biometric data (face maps or fingerprints). We only receive a "success" or "failure" signal from the system.

## 5. No Third-Party Tracking

Tally does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. We do not track your location, your usage patterns, or your identity.

## 6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally, the security of your information depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a passcode and enabling FaceID/TouchID for maximum protection.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
